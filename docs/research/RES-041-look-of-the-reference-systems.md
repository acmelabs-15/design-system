# RES-041: What the five reference systems define for the look

## Question
What do the five reference systems (Vercel's Geist, shadcn/ui, Radix Themes, Radix Primitives, Chakra UI) define for the look (colour, type scale, shape, spacing and sizes, elevation, motion, focus indication), what does Geist leave out that another of them defines, and what do Vercel's terms say about the reuse of Geist's values?

## Answer
Sufficient answer: for each system and each part of the look, what its own documentation or source defines today, with published values where they exist; a plain statement where a system defines nothing for a part, verified from its documentation; one list of each thing Geist does not define and at least one other system defines; and what Vercel publishes about reuse of the Geist design system's values (license, terms or statement, exact words; the Geist font license alone does not answer). Facts only, with version or access date per system, and what could not be confirmed.

Things Geist does not define and at least one other system defines. "Not in Geist" means absent from both the Geist pages and the site CSS; "not documented" means present only in the site CSS.

Not in Geist:
1. A density or scaling setting (Radix Themes `scaling` 90% to 110%).
2. A theme-wide radius setting or derived radius scale (Radix Themes `radius` none to full; shadcn/ui `--radius` with 7 steps; Chakra 11 radii).
3. Border-width tokens (Chakra borders 0.5px to 8px).
4. A choice of accent colour (Radix Themes `accentColor`; Chakra `colorPalette`).
5. A choice of tinted grays (Radix Themes 6 grays; shadcn/ui 7 base colours).
6. Alpha variants of the hue scales (Radix Colors gives one for every scale; Geist has gray-alpha only).
7. A named contrast method and a contrast guarantee (Radix Colors: APCA, "guaranteed to pass target contrast ratios").
8. A solid or translucent panel option (Radix Themes `panelBackground`).
9. A named duration scale (Chakra 7 durations).
10. Focus ring variants (Chakra outside, inside, mixed) and a focus colour that follows the component's colour (Radix Themes focus scale; Chakra per-palette `focusRing`).
11. A light weight of 300 (Radix Themes; Chakra 100 to 900).
12. Chart colour tokens (shadcn/ui chart-1 to chart-5) and sidebar tokens (shadcn/ui). Checked in the Geist docs only; the site CSS was not searched for chart colours.

Not documented by Geist (values exist only in the site CSS):
13. A spacing scale (Radix Themes, Chakra and Tailwind for shadcn/ui document one).
14. Control heights per size (Radix Themes, shadcn/ui, Chakra state them).
15. Status colour roles (Chakra error, warning, success, info; shadcn/ui destructive).
16. Motion curves and durations, and a reduced-motion rule (Chakra documents both; Radix Themes ships only no-preference animations).
17. The focus ring appearance (Radix Themes, shadcn/ui and Chakra publish it in docs or source).
18. Inset or inner shadows and an overlay colour (Radix Themes `--shadow-1`, `--color-overlay`; Chakra `inner`, `inset`).

Reuse of Geist's values: the Geist pages carry no license, copyright notice or reuse statement. The Brands page governs trademarks and logos and says a software license does not license a trademark. Vercel's Terms of Service (last updated October 19, 2026) grant a limited license "to use the Services for your internal business or personal purposes", forbid derivative works "based on the Services (except to the extent expressly permitted by Vercel or authorized within the Services)", and reserve "all intellectual property rights" in the Services; they do not name Geist or the documentation site. Exact words are in Findings. No legal conclusion is drawn here.

Not confirmed:
- Geist publishes no version, and the site CSS is the documentation site's build output, not a published Geist package; its values may change without notice, and some tokens (for example `--geist-success`, `accents-*`) look like older layers that the docs do not mention.
- The numeric APCA targets of Radix Colors.
- Whether Geist's Grid or Terminal pages define anything for the look; they were not read.
- Whether Vercel's Terms of Service apply to the Geist documentation pages; the terms do not say.
- shadcn/ui component sources were read from the new-york-v4 registry; the docs sidebar now links Base UI variants (`/components/base/`), whose classes were not compared.
