# Decision records

These records explain selected product choices, superseded choices and the evidence behind them. Their numbers are stable identifiers, not decision dates. A dated permission or completed review is history; it does not grant permission for a new task.

Read the [current status](../STATUS.md) and [active tasks](../../tasks/todo.md) for work in progress. The [design-system specification](../../spec/SPEC-design-system.md) owns current product contracts. Follow a record when the task touches its subject; this index does not require reading the whole collection.
