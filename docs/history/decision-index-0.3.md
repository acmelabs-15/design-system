# Decision records

These records explain selected product choices, superseded choices and the evidence behind them. Their numbers are stable identifiers, not decision dates. A dated permission or completed review is history; it does not grant permission for a new task.

This index covers the records of releases 0.1 to 0.3. For work in progress, follow [the workspace](../agents/workspace.md#session-state). The [design-system specification](../../specs/SPEC-003-design-system/spec.md) owns current product contracts. Follow a record when the task touches its subject; this index does not require reading the whole collection.
