Placeholder selected#

A value equal to the placeholder shows the placeholder as the value, in gray.

<acme-select aria-label="Fruit" placeholder="Select a fruit" value="Select a fruit">
  <option value="apple">Apple</option>
  <option value="orange">Orange</option>
  <option value="banana">Banana</option>
  <option value="grape">Grape</option>
</acme-select>

No suffix#

suffix="false" drops the chevron cell; the field keeps its right padding.

<acme-select aria-label="No suffix" placeholder="No suffix" suffix="false"></acme-select>

Label without casing#

bypass-casing keeps the label text as written.

<acme-select bypass-casing label="My label" placeholder="Without casing"></acme-select>
