Retry#

retry appends the Retry control under the title; a click on it dispatches acme-retry. retry-label names it for assistive technology.

<acme-error-card heading="Couldn't load deployments" retry retry-label="Retry loading deployments"></acme-error-card>
<script>root.querySelector("acme-error-card").addEventListener("acme-retry", () => console.log("retry"));</script>
