Controls#

The bar once the video can play: 48px, 85% wide, 5% above the bottom, transparent until the pointer moves over the player. Play, the elapsed time, the scrubber and the duration.

Controls visible#

While the pointer moves over the player the bar is opaque and lifted 6px; it hides three seconds after the last move, or when the pointer leaves. Here the video is playing.
