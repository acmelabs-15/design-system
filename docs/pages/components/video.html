Video

Embed a video with playback controls and lazy loading.

<acme-video>

Default#

With a src the element renders the player; the control bar floats over the frame.

<acme-video src="https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-videos/flower.mp4" style="display:block;max-width:600px"></acme-video>

No controls#

<acme-video controls="false" autoplay src="https://interactive-examples.mdn.mozilla.net/media/cc0-videos/flower.mp4" style="display:block;max-width:600px"></acme-video>

API#

Every reactive property reflects from its attribute; array and object values take JSON in the attribute. Events bubble and are composed.

<acme-video>AcmeVideo
