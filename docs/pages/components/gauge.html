Best Practices#

When to use

  • A 0–100 ratio where the comparison is the point: quota, cache hit rate, uptime. Progress for known task progress; Status Dot or Badge for a state.

Content

  • Always pair the gauge with a label naming the metric; thresholds match the product's breakpoints (80 warning, 95 error).
