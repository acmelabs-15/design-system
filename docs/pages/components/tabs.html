Best Practices#

When to use

  • Sibling views inside one page (Overview, Logs, Settings); a sub-menu for unrelated pages. Cap at 5–7 on desktop.

Behavior

  • Selection is instant and lives in the URL; a disabled tab gets a tooltip naming the constraint; Left/Right move focus.

Content

  • Title Case 1–2 word nouns; no counts in the title, a badge slot instead.
