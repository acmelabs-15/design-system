Best Practices#

When to use

  • Explain why something exists, not what it is; Context Card for an entity preview; never wrap a labelled Input.

Behavior

  • Opens on hover and focus after ~150ms; Escape closes; primary actions stay outside.

Content

  • One sentence or fragment, sentence case, no period; never repeat the visible label or describe the click.
