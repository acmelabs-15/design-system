Radii#

4 for kbd and chips, 6 for controls and cards, 8 for large inputs, 10 for chart panels, 12 for menus and modals, 16 for sheets, full for pills.

Best Practices#

When to use

  • One Material per element.
  • Pick the type from where the element sits in the layered hierarchy.
  • Prefer the lowest elevation that still reads.
  • Semantics live on the role-bearing wrapper.
  • Test both themes.
