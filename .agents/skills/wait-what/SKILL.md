Wait, I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that.

- Give me a little bit of context first: what this is about and where things stand.
- Talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: short sentences, one idea each, active voice, present tense.
- Use the ubiquitous language from `CONTEXT.md` (follow `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the right one if the repo has more than one). Plain words otherwise; expand a technical term the first time it appears.
- Lead with the point. Do not bury it in the premise.
- Organize it visually: a table for a choice, a list for a sequence or parallel items, short paragraphs. No large blocks of text.
- Keep every detail. The presentation changes; the depth does not. I still want the full substance of what you were telling me.
